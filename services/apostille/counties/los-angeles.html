Los Angeles County document requirements

How do Los Angeles County documents get apostilled?

Los Angeles County has a California Secretary of State walk-in office at 300 South Spring Street. It is one of two Secretary of State offices in California that issue Apostilles; the other is in Sacramento. Qualifying documents receive same-day service on a first-come, first-served basis.

  1. We verify that the document has the correct California official’s signature.
  2. We use the Los Angeles walk-in counter for same-day service or the Sacramento mail-in route for longer lead times.
  3. We return the apostilled document by tracked mail or courier to any destination worldwide.

Common Los Angeles County Apostille orders

Which Los Angeles County documents can be apostilled?

Vital records

California birth, marriage, death, and divorce certificates issued by Los Angeles County County Recorder or CDPH Vital Records. Certified copies with a raised seal are required — photocopies are not eligible.

Notarized documents

Powers of attorney, affidavits, consent letters, statutory declarations, and any other document signed in front of a California Notary Public. As a mobile Notary service, we notarize on-site anywhere in Los Angeles County and route the document straight to Apostille.

Corporate documents

California corporate resolutions, articles of incorporation, certificates of good standing, and officer certifications for foreign subsidiaries, joint ventures, and international contracting.

School records

Diplomas, degrees, and transcripts from California high schools, colleges, and universities. Registrar signatures typically need California Notary verification before Apostille — we coordinate the full chain.

Court records

California court judgments, divorce decrees, and certified court records with a Court Clerk certification. Adoption records, name changes, and judgment liens are common types.

International adoption support

LA County is one of the largest sources of adoption home studies and background checks bound for Hague and non-Hague adoption authorities abroad.

Entertainment industry contracts

Film, music, and licensing agreements signed in LA and used in Europe, Asia, or Latin America regularly require Apostille certification.

Higher-education transcripts & diplomas

Graduates of UCLA, USC, Loyola, Pepperdine, and dozens of other LA institutions frequently need Apostilled transcripts for foreign study or employment.

Los Angeles County Apostille FAQ

Los Angeles County Records Apostille FAQ

Where do I get a certified copy of a Los Angeles County birth, death, or marriage certificate?

The Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk keeps county birth records back to 1866 and issues certified copies in person by appointment or by mail. Mail requests for an authorized copy need a notarized Certificate of Identity, which we can notarize at your home or office. Births from the current or previous year are handled by the Los Angeles County Department of Public Health, except Pasadena and Long Beach, which keep their own records.

Can a Los Angeles Superior Court document be Apostilled?

Yes, when it is a certified copy from the court clerk with the clerk’s signature and the court seal. Divorce judgments, name change orders, adoption orders, and custody orders are common examples. Request the certified copy from the courthouse that holds the case file; our LA Superior Court filing reference lists where each case type is filed.

Do I have to drive to Sacramento to Apostille a Los Angeles County document?

No. The California Secretary of State issues Apostilles at its downtown Los Angeles office at 300 South Spring Street, with same-day service for qualifying documents. We handle the counter visit for you.

What documents from Los Angeles County can be Apostilled?

Any document signed by a California official the Secretary of State recognizes: Registrar-Recorder birth, death, and marriage certificates, certified Superior Court records, documents notarized by a California Notary Public, school transcripts and diplomas, and California business records. Federal documents, including FBI reports, USCIS certificates, and NARA records, follow a separate process through the U.S. Department of State.

Can you pick up my documents from my home or office in Los Angeles County?

Yes. We provide mobile pickup throughout Los Angeles County. We can notarize documents on-site, collect existing notarized documents or certified records, and manage the Apostille through final delivery.

Do I need consular legalization in addition to the California Apostille?

Only when the destination is not a Hague Apostille Convention member. Hague countries accept the Apostille by itself. Non-Hague countries may also require consular legalization at the destination country’s consulate. We coordinate that additional step when required.

Can I combine my Apostille order with Notary or translation services?

Yes. We can combine mobile Notary, certified translation, and Apostille service in one order. One team manages the handoffs between services.
