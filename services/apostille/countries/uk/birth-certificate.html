Document review

Which Birth Certificate Should You Submit?

Documents to send for review

  • Certified copy issued by California CDPH or a county vital records office
  • The version requested by the receiving UK authority
  • Document in final form — no corrections, no lamination
  • Matches the name and date of birth exactly as needed for the UK application

Common problems to resolve first

  • Hospital-issued birth announcements or keepsake certificates — not certified vital records
  • A certificate version that does not match the recipient’s instructions
  • Laminated documents that cannot be submitted for apostille processing
  • Photocopies — the apostille attaches to the original certified document
  • Expired or superseded certified copies replaced by a new filing

Birth Certificate Apostille FAQ — UK Use

Frequently asked questions.

Does the UK accept a California apostille on my U.S. birth certificate?

A California apostille can authenticate a California-issued birth certificate for UK use. Confirm whether the receiving UK authority requires an apostille before ordering service.

Do I need the long-form or short-form birth certificate for UKVI?

The required version depends on the UK application and receiving authority. Ask UKVI, the Home Office, or your immigration adviser whether a full certified copy is required.

My birth certificate is from a different state — can you still apostille it?

The issuing state’s apostille authority, not California’s, must authenticate that birth certificate. Contact that state’s authority for its current submission options.

I was born abroad to a U.S. parent — do I need the CRBA apostilled?

A Consular Report of Birth Abroad is a federal document. The U.S. Department of State in Washington, D.C., handles its apostille. Contact us to discuss U.S.-side support.

Can you apostille a UK birth certificate for me?

A UK-issued birth certificate uses the UK FCDO Legalisation Office. This page covers U.S.-issued documents and U.S.-side apostille services.

How long will UKVI take to process my application after I send the apostilled birth certificate?

Apostille and UK application timelines vary. Confirm current processing estimates with the issuing authority and the receiving UK organization before setting a deadline.
