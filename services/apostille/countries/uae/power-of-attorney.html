Document preparation

What Should You Confirm Before the Notary Visit?

Prepare these items

  • Final POA supplied or approved by the UAE recipient or your UAE lawyer
  • The recipient’s required powers, form, and authentication instructions
  • Grantor (donor) present with current government-issued photo ID
  • California acknowledgment block (most POAs use acknowledgment)

Avoid these problems

  • For a jurat, do not sign before meeting the notary
  • Do not use a generic form when the recipient requires specific language
  • Photocopies — the authentication chain requires the original notarized document

Power of Attorney Attestation FAQ — UAE Use

Frequently asked questions.

Does a California apostille work for UAE property transactions?

Requirements depend on the UAE recipient and transaction. Ask your UAE lawyer, developer, bank, or authority for written authentication instructions before choosing an apostille or attestation path.

Can I use an Arabic-language POA notarized in California?

The document may be in another language, but the notary must communicate directly with the signer and complete a California-compliant certificate. Ask the UAE recipient whether it requires a bilingual document or translation.

My UAE property developer says they need a “special power of attorney.” What does that mean?

A “special power of attorney” generally limits authority to a named transaction or purpose. Ask your UAE lawyer or developer’s legal team for the required language and template.

How do I authorize someone to deal with my UAE bank account from the U.S.?

Each UAE bank sets its own requirements. Ask the bank for its POA form, required powers, and authentication instructions before asking us to notarize or authenticate the document.

Can you deliver the authenticated POA directly to my UAE attorney-in-fact?

Yes. We can ship the document by tracked courier after completing the U.S.-side steps. Confirm who will handle any remaining UAE Embassy or MOFA steps.

Is a UAE MOFA-attested power of attorney valid indefinitely?

Validity depends on the document and receiving authority. Ask your UAE lawyer or recipient to confirm any expiration or recency requirement before notarization.
