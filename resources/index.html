Why we publish these

Our clients, attorneys, and title professionals ask the same questions every week: "Does Country X accept an Apostille?", "What will the county charge to record this deed?", "Which courthouse handles my case?" The answers change over time — countries join the Hague Convention, counties adjust fees, courts move departments. Most references we found were out of date, incomplete, or behind a paywall.

Our team needs current answers for internal work anyway. Publishing them as public tools is close to free for us and genuinely useful for anyone doing California document work. If you find an error or an outdated entry, tell us — we'll fix it and everyone benefits.

Every tool cites its source. The Hague country list draws from the HCCH status table, the fee calculator from the Los Angeles, Orange, and Ventura County recorder fee pages, and the court and facility directories from the courts and sheriff departments themselves.
